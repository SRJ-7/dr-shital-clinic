About Dr. Bolkuntwar

Compassionate Care, Expert Treatment

Dr. Shital Santosh Bolkuntwar is a highly qualified Ayurvedic physician with specialized expertise in treating diseases of the Eye and ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat). With an MS (AYU) from MUHS Nashik and a PhD from DY Patil University, Mumbai, Dr. Bolkuntwar combines traditional Ayurvedic wisdom with modern medical knowledge to provide comprehensive and holistic care to patients.

Dr. Bolkuntwar believes in personalized treatment plans that address the root cause of ailments while considering the patient's overall well-being. With over 15 years of experience, she has successfully treated thousands of patients suffering from various eye and ENT conditions using natural and effective Ayurvedic therapies.
